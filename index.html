Why Be In The Room
Not a guest lecture.
Not a webinar.

The Bespoke Show is a curated industry panel — 2 to 3 senior practitioners, moderated live by Sid Baliga, on a topic agreed in advance.

No motivational speeches. No recruitment pitches. No slide deck needed. Real practitioners talking about what actually works, what fails, and what they wish they knew earlier.

Students and faculty are the audience, and the conversation is calibrated to a B-School campus. The room leaves with something they can use.

Who We're Looking For
People who have done the work.

TBS panels work because the people in them have actually done the work — not just observed it. If you know someone whose thinking would shift how students see Sales or HR, we want to hear about them.

Self-nominations are equally welcome. If you think you belong in the room, make the case.

10+ years in Sales, HR, or adjacent leadership
Opinions earned through real experience — not theory
Can hold a room without a slide deck
